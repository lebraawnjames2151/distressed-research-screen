# Screen criteria

The team's own criteria. Claude tests each one and never changes them. Fill this in at the first session.
A hard limit is a number with a unit; a fail on any hard limit makes the result **no**.

## Return threshold

### Opportunities fund
- Target return: IRR above 15%. Yield to worst is used only as a quick first screen; the test is IRR.
- Holding period assumed: multi-year, 2 to 5 years. Screen shows IRR at 2, 3 and 5 years (to confirm).
- Yield-to-worst quick-screen level: not set yet

### CLO fund
- Focus: downside protection, not return. Loans bought at a small discount to par.
- Test: ability to service debt, and value coverage of the loan.
- Maximum purchase price: none; can be above par

## Hard limits

| Criterion | Limit | Unit | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net leverage, max (both funds) | 10.0 | times EBITDA (EBITDA definition to confirm) | Set at onboarding |
| Liquidity runway, min (both funds) | 12 | months; cash plus undrawn revolver against cash burn | Set at onboarding |
| Loan-to-value, max (CLO) | 60 | % of enterprise value, debt through the loan bought (EV basis to confirm) | Value coverage |
| Maturity wall (CLO) | 18 | months; no maturity inside this without a refinancing plan | Downside |

Not used, by choice: interest cover minimum, positive free cash flow, CLO maximum purchase price (can be above par).
