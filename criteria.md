## Return threshold

- Target return: IRR above 15%. Yield to worst is used only as a quick first screen; the test is IRR.
- Holding period assumed: multi-year, 2 to 5 years. Screen shows IRR at 2, 3 and 5 years (to confirm).
- Yield-to-worst quick-screen level: not set yet

### CLO fund
- Focus: downside protection, not return. Loans bought at a small discount to par.
- Test: ability to service debt, and value coverage of the loan.
- Maximum purchase price: not set yet
