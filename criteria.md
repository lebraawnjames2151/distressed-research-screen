# Screen criteria

The team's own criteria. Claude tests each one and never changes them. Fill this in at the first session.
A hard limit is a number with a unit; a fail on any hard limit makes the result **no**.

## Return threshold

### Opportunities fund
- Target return: IRR above 15%. Yield to worst is used only as a quick first screen; the test is IRR.
- Holding period assumed: multi-year, 2 to 5 years. Screen shows IRR at 2, 3 and 5 years (to confirm).
- Yield-to-worst quick-screen level: not set yet

### CLO fund
- Focus: downside protection, not return. Loans bought at a small discount to par.
- Test: ability to service debt, and value coverage of the loan.
- Maximum purchase price: none; can be above par

## Hard limits

| Criterion | Limit | Unit | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net leverage, max (both funds) | 10.0 | times EBITDA: company-adjusted less capitalised development/software costs; adjusted shown alongside (default, not yet confirmed) | Set at onboarding |
| Liquidity runway, min (both funds) | 12 | months; cash plus undrawn revolver against cash burn | Set at onboarding |
| Loan-to-value, max (CLO) | 60 | % of enterprise value, debt through the loan bought (EV = equity market cap + net debt on screen date; default, not yet confirmed) | Value coverage |
| Maturity wall (CLO) | 18 | months; no maturity inside this without a refinancing plan | Downside |

Red flag only, not a hard limit: interest cover below 1.5x EBITDA / cash interest (default, not yet confirmed).

Not used, by choice: interest cover as a hard limit, positive free cash flow, CLO maximum purchase price (can be above par).

## What we look for (not a hard limit)

- Hard assets with real value (value we can support, not book value alone).
- Recurring revenue that is sticky, with some perceived competitive moat (evidence: retention, churn, renewal terms, pricing history).
- Sponsor contributing new equity (committed or funded, not just stated support).
- Clear catalysts within 6 to 24 months.
- If free cash flow is negative: a credible path to positive FCF before liquidity runs out. Test: months to FCF breakeven (company plan or our case) against liquidity runway in months.
