Dye & Durham Announces Conclusion of Transformation Committee, and Announces Appointment of Interim CEO and New Board Chair

Published on August 31, 2026

  • Transformation Committee concluded
  • Angela Zhang appointed as new Chair of the Board
  • Todd Schulte appointed as interim CEO

TORONTO, Aug. 31, 2026 /CNW/ — Dye & Durham Limited (the “Company” or “Dye & Durham”) (TSX: DND), a leading provider of cloud-based legal practice management software, today announced the appointment of Todd Schulte as interim Chief Executive Officer and provided an update on other governance matters.

Leadership and Governance Update

Mr. Schulte has been appointed as the Company’s interim CEO effective immediately, while the Board search for a permanent CEO remains ongoing. Mr. Schulte most recently served as Dye & Durham’s Chief Operating Officer and brings more than 30 years of experience helping multi-product organizations simplify operations, drive efficiencies, improve consistency and deliver stronger customer outcomes.

The Transformation Committee, chaired by Tyler Proud, was established to hold the duties and responsibilities of the Office of the CEO on an interim basis while the Board advanced its search for permanent leadership. With Mr. Schulte’s appointment, the management responsibilities transfer to him and the Committee has been dissolved. The Board’s search for a permanent Chief Executive Officer continues.

Mary Filippelli, who served as Chair of the Board and as a member of the Transformation Committee, has stepped down from the Board. Angela Zhang, an existing director and member of the Transformation Committee, has been appointed Chair of the Board.

“Today’s appointment and the governance actions being announced today are an important step toward the stability and focused leadership this Company needs,” Ms. Zhang said. “The Board remains confident that with consistent execution across our product portfolio and markets, Dye & Durham can deliver meaningful value for all of its shareholders.”

Mr. Schulte said: “We have made substantial progress in a short period of time, and my focus will be on maintaining that pace, continuing to simplify the business, accelerating the use of AI and technology, and delivering stronger outcomes for our customers and shareholders.”

The Board thanks Ms. Filippelli for her significant contributions to the Company, and Mr. Proud for his work as Chair of the Transformation Committee.

ABOUT DYE & DURHAM LIMITED

Dye & Durham Limited provides premier practice management solutions empowering legal professionals every day, delivers vital data insights to support critical corporate transactions and enables the essential payments infrastructure trusted by government and financial institutions. The company has operations in Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, and South Africa.

Additional information can be found at www.dyedurham.com.
