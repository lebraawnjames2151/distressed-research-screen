## Business

- WD-40 Company develops and sells products that solve problems in workshops, factories and homes. It was founded in 1953 and is based in San Diego, California. [S1]
- Its shares trade on Nasdaq under WDFC. [S2]
- It sells in more than 176 countries and territories. [S3]
- Sales come from two product groups: maintenance products, and homecare and cleaning products. [S4]
- It reports three segments: the Americas; EIMEA; and Asia-Pacific. [S5]
- It had 753 employees as of the fiscal 2026 annual report. [S6]
